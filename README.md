## 🛣️ Baseline Characteristics & Problem Analysis

### Corridor Parameters
* **Section:** Wildenfelser Straße (S 283) between the Muldestraße junction and the city boundary near "Am Wasserturm"[cite: 6, 7].
* **Road Functional Classification:** Local Access Arterial / Built-up Main Urban Road (**HS IV** per **RASt 06** / **RIN**)[cite: 6, 7, 8].
* **Design Speed ($v_{85}$):** $50\text{ km/h}$ (Built-up section) / $70\text{ km/h}$ (Approach section near town limits)[cite: 6, 7].
* **Design Traffic Volume ($ADT_{2030}$):** 14,000 veh/24h with a Heavy Goods Vehicle (HGV/HDV) share of **7.8%**[cite: 6, 7, 8].

### Critical Deficiencies Identified
1. **Cycling Infrastructure Gap:** Complete absence of dedicated cycle tracks within the inner-city limits despite an existing high-quality two-way cycle path in Reinsdorf, creating a severe safety hazard along steep gradients[cite: 6, 7].
2. **Pedestrian Safety Vulnerabilities:** Inadequate sidewalk dimensions, lack of barrier-free tactile pavings, and missing protected crossing facilities[cite: 6, 7].
3. **Capacity & Turning Bottlenecks:** Lack of designated left-turn lanes causing significant queue buildup, rear-end collision risks, and delays for straight-through traffic[cite: 7].
4. **Environmental Impact:** High noise pollution levels and unbuffered traffic emissions adjacent to residential areas[cite: 8].

## 📐 Cross-Section Reorganization & Variant Comparison

To accommodate active transit within a constrained total width, the existing asymmetrical 3-lane cross-section was re-engineered.

### Existing Cross-Section
* **Carriageway:** 3 undivided lanes ($\approx 10.50\text{ m}$)
* **Sidewalks:** Narrow, non-compliant walkways ($1.50\text{ m} - 2.00\text{ m}$)
* **Cycling:** Mixed on-street traffic (Unsafe for steep inclines)[cite: 6, 7]

---

### Developed Design Variants

#### Variant 1: Unidirectional Cycle Lanes (On-Street / Advisory)
* **Carriageway:** $2 \times 3.25\text{ m} = 6.50\text{ m}$
* **Cycling:** Unidirectional advisory/marked cycle lanes ($1.80\text{ m}$ each side)
* **Pedestrians:** Sidewalks ($2.25\text{ m}$ north, $2.25\text{ m}$ south)
* **Drawback:** Higher stress levels for cyclists due to physical proximity to heavy freight vehicles.

#### Variant 2: Preferred Structural Redesign (Two-Way Cycle Track)
* **Total Right-of-Way Width:** $15.50\text{ m}$[cite: 7, 8]
* **Carriageway Width:** $6.50\text{ m}$ ($2 \times 3.25\text{ m}$ lanes)[cite: 7, 8]
* **Physical Separation:** $0.75\text{ m} - 1.00\text{ m}$ safety buffer with green strip / concrete curbing[cite: 7, 8].
* **Two-Way Cycle Track (South Side):** $3.25\text{ m}$ continuous paved path[cite: 7].
* **Northern Sidewalk:** $2.20\text{ m}$ clear width[cite: 7].
* **Southern Sidewalk:** $2.50\text{ m}$ clear width adjacent to cycle path[cite: 7].

## 🚦 Intersection Design & Signalization (RiLSA Standards)

### Priority Junction Realignment
* **Left-Turn Pockets:** Dedicated $3.00\text{ m}$ left-turn lanes introduced at critical junctions to isolate turning vehicles and eliminate rear-end collision hotspots[cite: 7].
* **Crossing Facilities:** Integrated zebra crossings (*Zebrastreifen*, StVO Sign 293), structural refuge islands ($2.50\text{ m}$ width), and synchronized cycle crossings (*Radfahrerfurten*).

### Signal Control & Phase Timing
* **Signal Parameters:**
  * Cycle Length ($T_c$): $60\text{ s} - 90\text{ s}$ adaptive program
  * Clearance & Intergreen Times ($t_{Z}$): Calculated using standard RiLSA formulas considering crossing distances and pedestrian walk speeds ($1.2\text{ m/s}$).
* **Intergreen Matrix:** Developed matrix ensuring complete clearance between conflicting vehicle-vehicle and vehicle-pedestrian streams[cite: 7, 8].
* **Blind/Visually Impaired Provisions:** Acoustic signals and tactile floor indicators integrated into signalized nodes[cite: 7].

---

## 🔊 Traffic Calming & Environmental Mitigation

* **Noise Abatement:** Application of low-noise asphalt (SMA LA) reducing rolling sound levels by up to $3\text{ dB(A)}$
* **Traffic Guidance:** Lane narrowing ($3.25\text{ m}$) induces natural speed adherence to $50\text{ km/h}$
* **Active Modal Shift:** Continuous green infrastructure buffers promote non-motorized trips, lowering local emissions

## 📑 German Standards & Technical Guidelines Applied

| Standard | Description | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RASt 06** | Richtlinien für die Anlage von Stadtstraßen | Road categorizing, cross-section dimensioning, safety widths
| **RiLSA** | Richtlinien für Lichtsignalanlagen | Signal timing, intergreen matrices, phase diagrams
| **RIN** | Richtlinien für integrierte Netzgestaltung | Network accessibility and functional staging
| **ERA** | Empfehlungen für Radverkehrsanlagen | Cycle track dimensions, buffers, crossing geometry |
| **EFA** | Empfehlungen für Fußgängerverkehrsanlagen | Barrier-free widths, tactile paving, sidewalk clearances |
| **StVO** | Straßenverkehrs-Ordnung | Markings, signage, and traffic priority rules |
